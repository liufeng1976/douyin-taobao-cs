# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, technical and evidence-oriented. Harassment, threats, discrimination, spam, credential harvesting and publication of other people's private data are not acceptable.

For this repository specifically:

- do not post real merchant secrets, access tokens, cookies or customer PII;
- do not submit fabricated claims of platform approval, production access, sales results or automated actions;
- use synthetic data for reproductions whenever possible;
- discuss disagreements around code, evidence and project boundaries rather than people.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content that creates security/privacy risk or materially violates these expectations.
